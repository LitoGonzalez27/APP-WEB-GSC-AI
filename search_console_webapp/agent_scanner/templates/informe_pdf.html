Is your site ready to be used by AI?

This report measures whether AI systems can read your site, understand it without getting it wrong, and act on it (buy, book, get in touch). This is not classic SEO: it is the layer that determines whether your brand exists when an agent does the work for the user. The first pages are for making decisions; the technical evidence at the end is meant to be handed straight to your tech team or agency.
