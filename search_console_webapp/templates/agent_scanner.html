Options

Render JavaScript
Compares what bots see with what a real browser sees. Adds about 2 minutes.
Read as Googlebot if we get blocked
Only used to verify the factors when no other method retrieves content. It does not change how we measure access: which AI bots get in is still measured with their real user-agent, and the block is reported all the same. Only enable it on your own domains or with the client's permission: impersonating Googlebot from a non-Google IP can get you blocked more, not less.
Real agentic tests check 6.3
ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude drive a real browser and attempt a task on your site and on your competitors' (cart and checkout with filled-in details, a form, or the cheapest plan). Nothing is ever paid and no accounts are created: card fields are blocked in code. It does not slow down the analysis: the report will show a “Simulate agents” button so you can run them whenever you like.
Attempts per agent
Agents are not deterministic: repeating the task tells “the site works” apart from “it got lucky that time”. Each attempt adds time.
I authorise submitting test forms
Only one real form is submitted during the whole analysis, and only on your domain: you will receive a notification in the name of “Test Auditoria”. Never on competitors, never payments, never accounts.
